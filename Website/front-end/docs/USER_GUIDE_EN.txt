ACCESS LONDON - USER GUIDE
Website: https://access-london-data-20261103.dbibd.chatgpt.site/

ONE WEBSITE, ONE EXPLORATION WORKSPACE
Use Explore map. Places, Stations and Facilities are views within the same page. The selected borough is retained when switching. The old Stations & facilities address redirects here.

1. PLACES
Choose Places, search or select a borough, then press Open [borough]. Search places, select a category and choose the access information you need. Select a list result or map point to see the source, dates and nearby stations. Need filters rank results: unknown and non-matching places remain visible. Saved places are local to the current device.

2. STATIONS
Choose Stations. Your borough remains selected. Choose All London boroughs to search more widely. Search by station name, filter by source access category and select a result. Read platform, boarding, lift and toilet details. Open Live lift reports and check the retrieval time. The current feed is requested every two minutes while this view is visible. If the shared server proxy is rate-limited, the browser tries the same official TfL feed directly and labels that connection. If both requests fail, current status is unavailable. No matching disruption does not confirm that all lifts are working.

3. FACILITIES
Choose Facilities. Filter entrances, lifts, ramps or toilets. Facility source selects OpenStreetMap, Toilet Map or both. Toilet wheelchair access filters the source's yes, partial, no or unknown answer. Toilet Map contributes 1,921 London records; 876 have a positive wheelchair-access claim. Inspect RADAR, payment, provider notes and source dates in the detail. Structured opening-time ranges are shown without weekday labels because the export does not document day order. Confirm the applicable days with the venue. Sources may overlap: the result count is not a unique toilet count. Nearby OSM candidates are unreviewed and are not automatically merged.

VENUE OPERATOR INFORMATION
Science Museum and V&A place details include a pilot of nine operator statements checked online on 8 November 2026. These remain separate from OSM tags and reviewed observations. The check date is not a survey date. Read any scope limits and source notices.

4. CONTRIBUTE
Open Contribute or use Share a dated observation from a place or facility detail. Sign in, select the correct record, choose the checked attribute and enter the answer, precise scope, date and evidence URL. Submit for review. Pending records are private; approved records become public. Original source tags remain unchanged. Reviewers cannot approve their own submissions.

LOCAL SURVEY
The Local survey option inside Places saves quick answers on this device only. Exporting local notes does not submit them. Use Contribute for online review and sharing.

INTERPRETING THE DATA
Yes supports a specific source field, not a guarantee of a fully accessible venue. No or Partly indicates a negative or limited source claim. Unknown means insufficient evidence. Reviewed observations are dated reports. Nearby distances are straight-line distances; no accessible route planning is provided.
