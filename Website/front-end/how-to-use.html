A LITTLE HELP BEFORE YOU GO

One map.
Your next step.

Access London helps you explore places, check station access and read facility evidence. Places, Stations and Facilities are three views of the same map workspace. You can browse without signing in.

Start exploring →
01 / FIND YOUR DESTINATION

Start with Places

  1. Open Explore map and choose Places.
  2. Search for a borough, or choose one from the borough grid, then press Open [borough]. You can also choose Use my location and allow location access.
  3. Search for a café, park, shop, museum or toilet. Choose a category and the access information you need.
  4. Select a result or map point to read its source tags, details, dates and nearby stations.

Access needs rank source-supported matches first; unknown and non-matching places remain visible. Step-free entrance currently has evidence for one venue: V&A. It covers two named entrances, not every entrance or a complete route. Every-floor access and Changing Places filters are unavailable while their evidence is missing. Use the mapped-lift and official-register links below the filters. Saved places are stored on this device.

02 / CHECK HOW TO ARRIVE

Switch to Stations

  1. Choose Stations above the map. Your selected borough stays selected.
  2. Search by station name, or change Area to All London boroughs.
  3. Select a station to read street-to-platform access, boarding information and recorded facilities.
  4. Open Live lift reports, or read the selected station’s current report section.

The TfL feed refreshes every two minutes while the Stations view is open. Always check its retrieval time. If the shared proxy is rate-limited, your browser tries the official TfL feed directly; the message identifies that connection. If both fail, current status is unavailable. No reported disruption does not confirm that every lift is working. Static station information is dated separately.

03 / READ THE ACCESS DETAILS

Switch to Facilities

  1. Choose Facilities in the same map workspace.
  2. Use the facility filter for entrances, lifts, ramps or toilets. Facility source selects OpenStreetMap, Toilet Map or both. Toilet wheelchair access selects a source answer.
  3. Select a result to inspect source claims, restrictions and dates. Toilet Map details include RADAR keys, payment information and provider notes. Confirm the applicable days for opening-time ranges.
  4. Where an exact place link exists, use Open this place on the main map to return to its Places detail.

The 1,921 Toilet Map records and 4,514 OSM objects may overlap. Counts describe source records, not unique toilets. These categories include positive, negative and restricted-access claims. A point in the list is not automatically a usable facility. An adult-changing candidate is not a verified official Changing Places facility.

04 / SHARE WHAT YOU KNOW

Contribute an observation

  1. From a place detail, follow its submission link, or open Contribute in the main navigation.
  2. Sign in, choose the correct place and select what you checked.
  3. Enter the answer, exact entrance or facility, observation date and evidence link.
  4. Choose Submit for review. Follow its status in My submissions.

Pending records are private to the contributor and reviewer. Approved observations become public. Reviewers cannot approve their own submissions. No observation overwrites the original source tags.

Read the labels before deciding

Label or informationWhat it means
Yes / Has itThe available source supports that particular field. Check its scope and date.
Partly / NoThe source records a limit or a negative answer. Read the exact detail.
Not known / UnknownThere is not enough evidence. It does not mean the feature is absent or accessible.
Live lift reportA current TfL feed response with a retrieval time; it is separate from static station access information.
Reviewed observationA dated contribution approved by a reviewer, still limited to the facility and time observed.

Useful to know

Are Places, Stations and Facilities separate websites?

No. They share the same website, map workspace, navigation and selected area. Use the three tabs in Explore map to move between them. Old station-page links now open the corresponding map view.

What is Local survey?

Inside Places, Local survey helps you work through missing information and save quick notes on your device. Those notes are not sent to the online database. To publish dated evidence for review, use Contribute. Local notes can be exported, but export does not submit them.

Does Access London plan a step-free route?

No. It shows source information and geographic points. Nearby-station distances are straight-line distances, not accessible walking routes or travel times. Check the relevant operator or venue for the full journey.

Why can a result be missing information?

The website displays the records available in its sources. Coverage varies. It does not claim to include every venue, entrance, lift or toilet in London. The official Changing Places registry and new field surveys are not yet connected.

Which data is current?

Core OSM places use the 18 October 2026 snapshot; OSM facility evidence uses 3 November 2026; Toilet Map uses the 8 November 2026 export. The static TfL access file was re-fetched on 8 November 2026, with its publisher modification date still 23 May 2025. Lift disruption reports are fetched separately from the current TfL API. Static datasets need future refreshes. Read About & data for full source notes.
